These Italian organisations host international volunteers and have accepted volunteers from outside the EU, including Arab countries. Most of them publish their places through the **European Solidarity Corps**, so its rules apply: read [Volunteering and other ways to come to Italy](../volunteering-and-pathways/) first. Current places are on the [Opportunities](../../opportunities/) page.

**Before you apply.** Each call lists the countries it accepts, and this changes from one call to the next. A call open only to Europeans today may be followed by one that accepts your country. Check the "Looking for participants from" list on the official page. Real volunteering programmes are free: **never pay** anyone who promises you a place or a visa.

**Visa.** For European Solidarity Corps volunteers, the Italian Youth Agency sends the names of incoming volunteers to the Italian consulate and requests the visa, under an agreement with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Acceptance makes your application stronger, but the consulate decides.

## YouNet APS – Bologna

A youth association working on European mobility, non-formal education and digital communication.

- **Programme:** European Solidarity Corps.
- **Current place (checked 27 November 2026):** digital communication and non-formal education, 24 weeks between June 2026 and December 2027, no application deadline.
- **Who can apply:** 18 to 30, living in Algeria, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Libya, Morocco, Palestine, Syria or Tunisia (and many European countries).
- **Tasks:** social media, videos, photos and blog posts for European projects.
- **What is covered:** a shared room with a volunteer of the same gender, a monthly food allowance and a bus pass for Bologna.
- **Apply:** [opportunity page on the European Youth Portal](https://youth.europa.eu/solidarity/opportunity/15987_en)

## Zō Centro Culture Contemporanee – Catania

A centre for contemporary culture in Sicily that produces theatre, dance, concerts, cinema, festivals and workshops.

- **Programme:** European Solidarity Corps.
- **Current place (checked 27 November 2026):** one year, from 11 February 2027 to 10 February 2028. Apply by **9 December 2026**.
- **Who can apply:** 18 to 30, living in Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Libya, Morocco, Palestine, Syria, Armenia, Azerbaijan or Georgia (and European countries). Algeria and Tunisia are not on the list.
- **Tasks:** supporting cultural programming, education and social innovation activities.
- **What is covered:** accommodation and meals, and travel costs are reimbursed.
- **Apply:** [opportunity page](https://finderasmus.eu/projects/one-year-in-a-cultural-centre-in-catania), with the application form and the infopack.

## SCI Italia (Servizio Civile Internazionale) – Rome

The Italian branch of Service Civil International, one of the oldest volunteering movements, present in almost 100 countries.

- **Programmes:** long-term European Solidarity Corps places in Rome, and international workcamps of 7 days to 3 weeks.
- **Current places:** none open on 27 November 2026. The last calls closed in July and August 2026.
- **Who could apply:** some past calls accepted volunteers from Palestine.
- **What past calls covered:** accommodation in a shared flat, €350 a month of pocket money, visa costs up to €100 and travel under the programme's rules.
- **Language:** good English; Italian is a plus.
- **Follow:** [organisation page on the European Youth Portal](https://youth.europa.eu/volunteering/organisation/53789_en)

## CESIE – Palermo

A non-profit organisation in Sicily working on social inclusion with children, migrants, people with disabilities and young people not in education or work.

- **Programme:** European Solidarity Corps, long-term and short-term places.
- **Current places:** the latest call (December 2026 to August 2027) was open to European countries only and closed on 31 October 2026.
- **What is usually covered:** a flat in the city centre with a shared room, meals, and an optional 40-hour Italian course.
- **Follow:** [latest call on the European Youth Portal](https://youth.europa.eu/solidarity/opportunity/54815_en)

## Lunaria – Rome

A non-profit association working on international voluntary service since 1992. It hosts more than 300 foreign volunteers in Italy every year.

- **Programmes:** short international workcamps, youth exchanges, and medium and long-term volunteering.
- **Who can apply:** some workcamps are funded by the European Solidarity Corps and open to Europeans only; others are open through Lunaria's partner organisations abroad.
- **How to apply:** through a sending organisation in your country (see the next section).
- **Follow:** [Lunaria](https://www.lunaria.org/english/)

## Associazione ALA – Roccantica

An association working with young people in the rural Sabina area, about 40 km from Rome.

- **Programme:** European Solidarity Corps, short and long-term places in the countryside.
- **Current place (checked 27 November 2026):** Leave a Mark, 8 to 23 February 2027, apply as soon as possible.
- **Who can apply:** 18 to 30, living in Georgia, Moldova, the Western Balkans or the EU. It does not accept Arab countries.
- **Tasks:** outdoor manual work, such as building and marking mountain trails.
- **What is covered:** accommodation and meals, and travel costs are reimbursed.
- **Apply:** [opportunity page](https://finderasmus.eu/projects/leave-a-mark-in-the-nature-of-rural-italy-roccantica)

## International workcamps

A workcamp is group volunteering of one to three weeks with volunteers from several countries, usually with a short-stay Schengen visa. It is also an option if you live in Africa or Asia, where the European Solidarity Corps is not open.

- **Italian organisations:** SCI Italia and Lunaria (above), Legambiente, YAP Italia.
- **How to apply:** you do not apply to the Italian organisation directly. You apply through a **sending organisation** in your country that is a member of [CCIVS](https://www.ccivs.org/) or the [Alliance](https://www.alliance-network.eu/international-volunteering/workcamps/). In Morocco, for example: [Chantiers Jeunesse Maroc](https://www.salto-youth.net/tools/otlas-partner-finding/organisation/association-chantiers-jeunesse-maroc-cjm.21353/) and [CSM Maroc](https://youth.europa.eu/volunteering/organisation/49434_en). If there is none in your country, the Alliance website explains what to do.
- **Costs:** you usually pay your travel and a registration fee to the sending organisation; the host gives you accommodation and food.

## Short Erasmus+ trainings and youth exchanges

Training courses and seminars of a few days, organised by the Erasmus+ National Agencies and SALTO EuroMed. Many take place in Italy and are open to the South Mediterranean countries. Some cover accommodation and training, and travel and visa costs. They are mostly for people active in youth organisations.

Search the [SALTO European Training Calendar](https://trainings.salto-youth.net) and choose "Southern Mediterranean countries".

## What we do not recommend

Platforms such as Workaway or WWOOF and paid "voluntourism" programmes. They do not support your visa application: you travel on a tourist visa, which makes your application weaker.
