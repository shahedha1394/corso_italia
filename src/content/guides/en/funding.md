## In short

- The biggest help for most students is the **regional DSU scholarship**: money, and sometimes a room and meals.
- It depends on your **family income**, with limits that differ from region to region. International students usually need an **ISEE parificato**, or a certificate from the Italian embassy if they come from a particularly poor country.
- The **Italian government (MAECI)** also offers scholarships through the Study in Italy portal. The 2026/2027 call closed in March 2026.
- To receive the money you need a **bank account in your name**, usually in Italy (see section 6).
- Calls close early, often **before classes start**. Check the dates as soon as you choose your university.

## 1. Regional DSU scholarships

Each region funds scholarships for students enrolled in its universities, including international students. Depending on the region, the call is run by a regional DSU agency or by the university itself, and each region sets its own income limits and amounts. For example, the University of Verona's 2026/2027 call (Veneto region) uses:

- Family **ISEE up to €26,306.25** and **ISPE up to €43,125.94**.
- For students who live away from their family: **€8,246 per year** for the lowest incomes, **€7,171.11** in the middle band, and gradually less (down to **€3,585.50**) near the income limit.

You also need to pass a minimum number of credits each year to keep the scholarship. In Tuscany, Lazio and Emilia-Romagna, for example, you cannot get it if you already hold a degree of the same level (a second master's, say). Read your region's call carefully.

## 2. The ISEE parificato

If your family's income and property are abroad, you need an **ISEE parificato**, issued in Italy by an authorised tax assistance centre (**CAF**) using documents from your country. Students from the ministry's list of **particularly poor countries** need a certificate from the Italian embassy instead. Start early: documents from your country may need translation and legalisation.

## 3. Italian government scholarships (MAECI)

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs offers scholarships to foreign students, selected by citizenship. You apply only through [Study in Italy](https://studyinitaly.esteri.it). The 2026/2027 call closed on **26 March 2026**, so check the portal early for the next one.

Who can apply, according to the 2026/2027 call:

- **Not if you study or have studied in Italy**: at a secondary school, a university or AFAM institution, or a foreign university based in Italy. Living in Italy is not excluded by itself.
- **Age at the deadline:** up to 28 for master's and AFAM courses, 30 for a PhD, 40 for research.
- **Language:** B2 in Italian or English, depending on the course. Not needed for a PhD or research.
- University **bachelor's degrees are not covered**.

The ministry also runs **Invest Your Talent in Italy**, for citizens of 23 countries (including Egypt and Tunisia) who live there permanently, born in 2000 or later: €10,800 for 9 months. It is not for people already studying at an Italian university or who hold an Italian degree. See the [scholarships directory](../scholarships/).

## 4. University scholarships and fee waivers

Many universities have their own scholarships or lower fees for international students. Look at the "scholarships" or "fees" page of each university.

## 5. Other funding

Some governments, foundations and international organisations fund study abroad. Ask in your country too.

## 6. Receiving the money: you need a bank account

Every scholarship paid in money goes to a bank account in your name. Where that account can be depends on the scholarship:

| Scholarship | Account needed to receive the money |
|---|---|
| Italian government (MAECI) | An account at an Italian bank in your name, plus an Italian codice fiscale |
| Invest Your Talent in Italy | An Italian bank account |
| DSU in Liguria (Aliseo) | An account opened in Italy, in your name or joint |
| DSU in Lazio (DiSCo) | An account in Italy or in a SEPA country, in your name or joint |
| DSU in Emilia-Romagna (ER.GO) | An account, or a named prepaid card with an IBAN, in your name. IBANs outside SEPA are not accepted |
| DSU in Piedmont (EDISU) | A SEPA IBAN in your name or joint |
| DSU in Tuscany | An account in your name |
| PhD scholarship | Your bank account. The University of Brescia accepts an account in a European country |
| Housing, meals and fee waivers | No account needed: these are services, not money |

- SEPA covers the EU and some other European countries (for example Switzerland, the United Kingdom, Norway and Serbia). It does not include any Arab country. An account in your home country will not work, so plan to open an account in Italy after you arrive.
- Without your bank details the agency cannot pay you (EDISU says so explicitly).
- The rules change every year: read the call (bando) of your region or university.
