---
title: "EngPro code of conduct and safeguarding"
---

EngPro is free for everyone. Teachers volunteer their time and learners never pay.

## For everyone
- Be respectful and patient. Everyone is here to learn or to help.
- No harassment, discrimination, hate speech or sexual content.
- No selling, advertising or asking for money.
- Keep personal contact details private. Use the session links we publish.

## For volunteer teachers
- Every volunteer is reviewed before their first session.
- Run sessions in group calls or public channels. Do not ask learners for private one-to-one contact.
- Learners must be 18 or older. If you think a learner is under 18, stop the session and tell us.
- Do not record a session without asking everyone first.

## Reporting a problem
If something makes you uncomfortable, leave the session and contact us through the Contact page. We will review every report and can remove anyone who breaks these rules.
