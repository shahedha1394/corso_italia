---
title: "Privacy"
---

This website does not use tracking cookies and does not collect personal data itself.

Sign-up forms for EngPro and for submitting opportunities are hosted on Google Forms. When you fill one in, your answers are stored by Google and seen only by the Corso Italy team, who use them to organise EngPro sessions and review submissions. Ask us through the Contact page to see or delete your data.

To know how many people use the site and which pages help them most, we count visits with GoatCounter. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal data: we only see totals, such as the number of visits per page, the country and the type of device.

Videos on this site come from YouTube. Until you press play, we only show a thumbnail image; the YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com) loads only when you press play, and from then on YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
