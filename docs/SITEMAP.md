# Site map and content outline

Every page exists in two languages, served from `/en/...` and `/ar/...`.
Arabic pages use `dir="rtl"`. The language switcher keeps the user on the same page.

## 1. Home (`/`)
- Short intro: "Study in Italy, step by step", in Arabic and English
- Three entry cards: **Study in Italy**, **EngPro**, **Opportunities**
- "Start here" journey: Choose a program → Apply → Visa → Arrive → Settle
- Latest announcements (3 newest, not expired)
- Upcoming deadlines (from the calendar)

## 2. Study in Italy (`/study`)

### 2.1 University directory (`/study/universities`)
- List of universities with city, type (public/private) and website
- University detail page (`/study/universities/[slug]`): short description, main fields, city, website, international office, link to the official program list

### 2.2 Study by city (`/study/cities`)
- One card per city: region, short description, universities in that city
- Individual study programs are not listed on this site; each university page links to the university's official program list, and Universitaly's official search covers all programs

### 2.3 How to apply (`/study/apply`)
- General guide that applies to all programs:
  1. Choose programs and check requirements
  2. Prepare documents (diploma, transcripts, translations, CV, motivation letter, recommendation letters, language certificate, passport)
  3. Academic recognition: **CIMEA statement of comparability** or **Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV)**
  4. Admission tests: IMAT, TOLC, SAT, university-specific tests
  5. Apply to the university (own portal, deadlines)
  6. **Universitaly pre-enrollment** (mandatory for non-EU students)
  7. Acceptance letter → visa

### 2.4 Funding (`/study/funding`)
- Regional **DSU** scholarships (per region: Lazio DiSCo, Lombardy, Piedmont EDISU, Tuscany DSU, ...)
- **ISEE parificato** guide (needed for DSU and reduced fees)
- Italian government scholarships (MAECI "Invest your Talent", "Study in Italy" scholarships)
- University merit scholarships and tuition waivers
- External funding: home-country governments, foundations, Erasmus+
- Each entry: provider, eligibility, amount, deadline, link, last-checked date

### 2.5 Language requirements (`/study/language`)
- English-taught programs: IELTS / TOEFL / Duolingo / exemptions
- Italian-taught programs: CILS / CELI / PLIDA levels

### 2.6 Visa (`/study/visa`)
- Type D study visa: who needs it, when to apply
- Documents checklist (acceptance + Universitaly summary, proof of funds, accommodation, insurance, flight booking, passport photos)
- **Country selector**: embassy / VFS centre, booking links and local notes per country (start with Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, Tunisia, Algeria, Lebanon, Iraq, Syria, Palestine, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Sudan)
- Common reasons for refusal

### 2.7 Deadlines calendar (`/study/calendar`)
- Pre-enrollment windows, test dates, DSU calls, visa timing

## 3. Living in Italy (`/living`)
- **First weeks checklist**: what to do in the first 8 days, codice fiscale, SIM, bank account, transport card
- **Residence permit** (permesso di soggiorno): post office kit, questura appointment, fingerprints, collection, renewal
- **Health insurance**: private policy vs. enrolment in SSN, choosing a doctor (medico di base)
- **Accommodation**: university residences, private rooms, contracts and registration, avoiding scams, average prices per city
- **Cost of living** by city
- **Working while studying**: 20 hours/week rule, contracts, tax code
- **After graduation**: permit conversion, job-seeking permit
- **Glossary** of bureaucracy terms (Italian / Arabic / English)

## 4. EngPro (`/engpro`)
Separate section, own landing page and look, targeted at learners and volunteer teachers. 100% free.
- About the initiative and code of conduct
- **For learners**: placement self-test (CEFR A1–C2), free resource library by level and skill, sign-up form
- **For volunteer teachers**: how it works, sign-up form, short review before approval, safeguarding policy
- **Sessions**: list of upcoming group sessions (topic, level, time with timezone, link)
- Matching learners to volunteers by level and availability (admin-managed at first)

## 5. Opportunities (`/opportunities`)
- Announcements of workshops, teaching sessions and other opportunities
- Filters: type, online/in-person, city, date
- Each item: title, organiser, date, place/link, deadline, expiry date (hidden automatically after expiry)
- "Submit an opportunity" form → admin approval
- Optional: auto-post to a Telegram / WhatsApp channel

## 6. Shared pages
- About us / team, Contact, FAQ, Disclaimer ("information only, always check official sources"), Privacy policy (GDPR), Terms

## Content rules (apply to every guide)
- Written in both languages; an English page is not published without its Arabic version (or a visible "translation coming" note)
- Each guide shows a **last checked** date and links to official sources
- Plain language, short steps, checklists over long paragraphs
