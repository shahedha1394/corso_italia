Living in Italy

Finding accommodation

University residences, private rooms, contracts and how to avoid scams.

Last checked: 29 October 2026

In short

  • For the visa you must show suitable accommodation in Italy. An application for a student residence alone is not enough.
  • Student residences are run by the regional DSU agencies and some universities. Places are limited, and you apply through the yearly call.
  • A rental contract must be registered with the Agenzia delle Entrate within 30 days of signing.
  • Whoever hosts you or rents you a room must tell the police within 48 hours (dichiarazione di ospitalità).
  • Never pay before you have seen the room (in person or by video call) and the contract.

Where to look

  1. Your region’s DSU agency: student residences, often linked to the DSU scholarship. See the scholarships guide.
  2. Your university’s housing office: many have lists of rooms or partner residences.
  3. Private rooms: websites, student groups and agencies. Prices depend a lot on the city and the area.

For your first weeks, a hotel or short stay gives you time to visit rooms in person.

Your contract

  • Ask for a written contract and check how long it lasts, the deposit, and what the rent includes (bills, internet).
  • The contract must be registered within 30 days. Ask the landlord for proof of registration: you may need it for your residence permit.
  • The owner must send the hospitality declaration to the police within 48 hours. Keep a copy.

Avoiding scams

  • Do not send money by transfer or money service to someone you have not met, for a room you have not seen.
  • Be careful with prices much lower than others in the same area, and with owners who are “abroad” and cannot show the room.
  • Check that the person is the owner or a real agency, and pay the deposit only with a signed contract.
  • If in doubt, ask your university’s housing office.

Official sources

  • MUR circular for international students, academic years 2026/2027 and 2027/2028 (copy published by the University of Ferrara)
  • Agenzia delle Entrate: registering a rental contract (brochure)
  • University of Bergamo: hospitality declaration form (example)
