PhD calls by university

When each university's PhD call closed in the last cycle (42nd cycle, starting in autumn 2026), how much the scholarship is, and whether you can have the interview online.

These are last year's dates.

Each university publishes a new call every year, usually between January and September, and the dates change. Use this list to know roughly when to look, then follow the university's official page. Some universities have a separate call for each programme: the dates here are from the call we checked.

PhD places for students at risk

The University of Padova offered 3 PhD scholarships in the 42nd cycle for candidates at risk because of conflict, persecution or human rights violations, including refugees and asylum seekers. Some other universities reserve places for holders of foreign degrees or of scholarships from their own government: check each call.
