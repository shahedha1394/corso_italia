Your year at a glance

When each stage usually happens if you want to start in September or October. The stages overlap, so start early.

SeptOctNovDecJanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSeptOctNov

  1. 1 Explore and prepare

    September – December

    Choose universities and cities, check the language requirements and start preparing for tests.

  2. 2 Apply and take tests

    November – April

    Many universities open applications and pre-assessment, and most admission tests take place.

  3. 3 Admission and pre-enrolment

    March – August

    Universities send their answers. Prepare your documents and pre-enrol on Universitaly.

  4. 4 Study visa

    June – October

    Book your consulate appointment early. Some tests, such as IMAT, are still in September.

  5. 5 Arrive and settle in

    September – November

    Enrol at the university and request your residence permit within 8 working days of arriving.
