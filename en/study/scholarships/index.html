Italian government

Invest Your Talent in Italy

Scholarship programme of the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs for students from selected countries who enrol in master's and postgraduate courses taught in English (engineering and technology, architecture and design, economics and management) at participating Italian universities. The 2026/27 call closed on 11 May 2026.

Who can apply: Students from the countries listed in the current call. Check the call for the nationality or residence and age conditions.

Italian Government scholarships (MAECI)

Scholarships from the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs for foreign students studying in Italy. Applications are made only through the Study in Italy portal. The 2026/27 call closed on 26 March 2026.

Who can apply: Citizens of the countries on the eligible-countries list published on the portal.

Regional (DSU)

Tuscany

DSU Toscana scholarship

Regional right-to-study (DSU) scholarship. It is means-tested and can include money, a place in a residence and meals. International students usually need an ISEE parificato. Check the yearly call for amounts and deadlines.

Who can apply: Students enrolled at a university in the region who meet the income and merit requirements of the call.
